Southern Heating and AC: Website, SEO & Digital Footprint

A long-term web design, SEO, and content relationship with a Tampa Bay HVAC company — including how we've adjusted the approach as AI search has changed how people find local businesses.

Case Study · HVAC

Southern Heating and AC case study

← Back to all case studies

The Client

Southern Heating and AC is an HVAC company serving the greater Tampa Bay area. Like most home service businesses, the vast majority of their new customers find them online, so their website and how they show up in search results are directly tied to how many calls they get.

We started with a full website build and have stayed on as their ongoing web, SEO, and content team since — an active, continuing partnership rather than a one-time project.

The Website

We designed and built their site from the ground up, with a focus on the things that actually matter for an HVAC company: clear service pages, easy ways to call or request service, fast load times, and a design that looks credible to someone comparing a few companies before they pick up the phone.

Ongoing SEO

We manage their local SEO on an ongoing basis — the technical side of the website, the content, their Google Business Profile, and the overall footprint that determines whether they show up when someone nearby searches for HVAC service. This isn't a one-time setup. Search changes constantly, so we keep working on it as conditions shift.

Actively Managing the Website — Including for AI Search

One of the more significant shifts in the relationship has been adapting to AI-driven search. As tools like Google's AI Overviews and AI chat assistants have become part of how people research local services, we've had to rethink parts of how the site is structured and written so it holds up in that environment, not just in a traditional list of blue links.

Practically, that's meant writing content that directly and clearly answers the questions homeowners are actually asking, structuring pages so both search engines and AI tools can easily understand what the business does and where it operates, and keeping technical SEO fundamentals in good shape so the site stays eligible to be pulled into those AI-generated answers in the first place. This is an ongoing area of focus — the way people search is still changing, and we adjust the site along with it.

Social Media & Content

We shoot photo and video content for Southern Heating and AC's social media and marketing — technician work, completed jobs, and the kind of everyday content that builds trust with homeowners before they've ever called. Consistent, recognizable content across their social channels reinforces the same credibility their website is trying to establish.

Managing Their Digital Footprint

Beyond the website itself, we manage the broader digital footprint that affects how Southern Heating and AC shows up online — keeping business information consistent across directories and listings, and making sure their Google Business Profile stays active and accurate. For a service area business, consistent information across the web is a real part of maintaining trust and rankings over time.

The throughline across all of it is the same: the website, the SEO, the content, and the digital footprint aren't separate projects. They all feed each other, and managing them together is what keeps the results compounding.

Why This Works

Southern Heating and AC is a good example of what an ongoing web and marketing partnership looks like when it's working: the website, the SEO, the content, and the digital footprint all move together, staying active and responsive to changes as search itself evolves.
