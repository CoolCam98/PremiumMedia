Video Production for Attractions & Tourism

Photo and video content, social clips, and cable commercials for Florida attractions that need to look as fun on screen as they are in person.

Families Decide Before They Ever Visit

An attraction lives and dies by how well it’s represented. Families are deciding where to spend their day and their money based on what they see online and on TV, and the photos and video have to capture what a visit actually feels like.

We shoot on site, around your hours and your guests, and turn one shoot day into content for your website, social media, and advertising.

Our Work With Dinosaur World

We’re the ongoing media team for Dinosaur World, the walk-through prehistoric park in Plant City and one of the area’s best-known family attractions. For them we:

  • Shoot photo and video on site throughout the year, including seasonal content tied to their events
  • Cut short clips made for social media, where the first two seconds matter most
  • Handle branding and graphic design that shows up from signage to social posts
  • Produce commercials and place them on local cable, timed around school breaks and holidays
