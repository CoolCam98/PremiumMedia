Video production in Tampa, explained

Looking for a video production company in Tampa and not sure where to start? Here's what the process actually looks like, what it costs, and what you get at the end.

What does a video production company in Tampa actually do?

A video production company handles a video project from concept through final delivery — planning the idea, filming with proper cameras, lighting, and audio equipment, then editing, adding graphics or color correction, and exporting the final files in whatever format you need. In Tampa specifically, that often also means knowing how to get a finished commercial placed on local cable or streaming platforms.

What types of videos can a Tampa video production company create?

  • TV and streaming commercials
  • Corporate, training, and internal business videos
  • Event coverage and livestreaming
  • Interviews, testimonials, and podcasts
  • Short-form social media content
  • Drone aerial footage

Most production companies, including ours, can handle all of these in-house rather than farming pieces out to subcontractors. See our full video & production services for more detail on each.

How much does video production cost in Tampa?

Cost depends heavily on scope — a 30-second commercial with a half-day shoot costs very differently than a multi-location training series or a full event livestream. Rather than quoting a single number that wouldn't apply to most projects, we look at what you actually need and give you a clear cost and timeline before anything is booked.

How long does it take to produce a video?

A simple commercial or short-form video can often go from concept to delivery in a couple of weeks. Larger projects — multi-day shoots, complex editing, or anything needing client review rounds — take longer. We give you a realistic timeline upfront once we know the scope.

What's the difference between videography and video production?

Videography usually refers to just the filming — showing up and capturing footage, often for events. Video production covers the full process: concept, scripting, filming, editing, graphics, and final delivery. Many projects need full production, but some, like event coverage, are really just videography. We offer both depending on what the project calls for.

Do I need a script or concept before hiring a video production team?

No. Most clients show up without a script or even a fully formed idea, and that's normal — figuring out the concept is part of the process. We walk you through what to expect on shoot day, help shape the idea into something that works on camera, and handle the rest.

Can you produce commercials for TV and streaming in the Tampa market?

Yes. We produce 15, 30, and 60-second commercials with TV and streaming/OTT-ready exports, and we can help get the finished spot placed on local cable, broadcast, and streaming platforms like Hulu and YouTube so it actually reaches Tampa-area customers. More on this in our commercial production page.

Filming across the Tampa Bay area: We're based in Plant City and film on-site across Tampa, Lakeland, and the rest of Central Florida — at your business, an event venue, or wherever the project calls for.

Do you film on location in Tampa, or do I need to travel to a studio?

We film on-site across Tampa, Plant City, Lakeland, and the rest of Central Florida — at your business, at an event venue, or wherever the project calls for. We also have studio space available for projects like interviews or podcasts that benefit from a controlled environment.

What do I get at the end of a video production project?

You get the final edited video files in the formats you need — for example, a full-length version plus shorter cuts for social media — along with any graphics, color correction, and audio mixing baked in. For commercials, that includes broadcast and streaming-ready exports.
