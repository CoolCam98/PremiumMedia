Agritourism: U-Pick Farms, Pumpkin Patches & Festivals

Strawberry picking, pumpkin patches, corn mazes, Christmas tree farms, and fall festivals each have a short season to fill. We film your farm ahead of opening day, cut short videos for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok, and produce TV commercials placed on Spectrum and local streaming while families are making weekend plans.

We also cover festival days and events, including the Florida Strawberry Festival Parade in Plant City. For larger family destinations, see our attraction and theme park video production.

Agriculture Video Questions

Can you film during harvest without getting in the way?

Yes. We plan shoot days around your harvest, packing, and shipping schedule, and we coordinate access and timing with your farm manager so filming fits into the workday.

When should a U-pick farm or pumpkin patch start promoting its season?

We recommend planning about two months before opening day. That gives us time to film, cut social videos, and get a commercial on TV before families start making plans.

Do you make TV commercials for farms, festivals, and agritourism?

Yes. We produce the commercial and can place it on Spectrum cable and local streaming, timed to your season. See our commercial production page for more.

Do you offer drone footage of farms and groves?

Yes. We're licensed for drone work and can capture fields, groves, greenhouses, and facilities from the air.

Where do you film?

We're based in Plant City and film most often around Dover, Lithia, Lakeland, and across Hillsborough and Polk County. We travel farther for larger projects.
