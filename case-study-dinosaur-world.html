The Client

Dinosaur World is a national chain of walk-through prehistoric theme parks, with locations in Plant City, Florida, Glen Rose, Texas, and Cave City, Kentucky. The Florida park is one of the area's best-known family attractions and draws visitors from across Central Florida and beyond. It's the kind of business that lives and dies by how well it's represented — the photos and video have to actually capture what a visit feels like, the branding has to hold up next to national attractions competing for the same families, and the advertising has to reach people who are already planning a day out.

We've been Dinosaur World's media partner for more than five years, filming across all three parks and handling content production, day-to-day media management, branding, and cable advertising. They've kept it all with one team, which keeps everything consistent and lets us handle the full picture together.

Content Production

We regularly shoot on-site at the park — photo and video content that gets used across their website, social channels, and advertising. That includes seasonal content tied to their calendar of events, general park footage that shows the scale and detail of the exhibits, and shorter clips built specifically for social media, where attention spans are short and the first two seconds matter most.

Because we shoot for them on an ongoing basis, we know the park, we know what's changed since the last visit, and we know what's already been used, so new content stays fresh. That familiarity saves time on every shoot day and keeps the content library feeling current.

The content we shoot at the park earned more than 14 million views on Facebook and Instagram over the past year, reaching an audience of over 73,000 followers.

Media Management

Beyond producing content, we help manage where it goes and how it's used. That means organizing the library of photo and video assets so the right piece is easy to find when it's needed, keeping their online presence active with regularly refreshed content, and making sure what's public actually reflects the current state of the park — current hours, current exhibits, current events.

Ongoing media management is how we keep Dinosaur World's marketing current and accurate over time. Over the past year, visits to the Dinosaur World website grew more than 20%, and holiday-season traffic was up over 55% from the year before.

Branding

We've also worked on branding and design elements for Dinosaur World — the kind of graphic design work that shows up everywhere from signage to social posts to printed materials. Consistent branding matters more for an attraction than most businesses realize, because families are deciding whether to trust a park with their kids and their afternoon based on how put-together it looks before they ever walk through the gate.

Cable Advertising

We help place Dinosaur World's commercials on local cable, getting their content in front of the households most likely to actually visit. This is where content production and media buying meet — the footage we shoot throughout the year becomes the raw material for commercials that run seasonally, timed around school breaks, holidays, and the times of year families are actively looking for things to do nearby.

Handling the content and the cable placement together means the commercials always reflect what the park actually looks like right now.

The value of this kind of relationship comes from continuity — content, branding, and advertising all working from the same shared understanding of the park, the brand, and the goals.

Why This Works

Dinosaur World is a good example of what we do best: serving as the ongoing media team for a business, with content, branding, and advertising all informing each other under one roof. That keeps the work consistent, keeps turnaround fast, and gives their team one cohesive relationship instead of several separate ones.
