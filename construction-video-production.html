Construction & Industrial Video Production

Progress documentation, drone footage, safety and training video, and marketing content for construction, infrastructure, and industrial companies across Plant City, Tampa, and Lakeland.

Show the Scale of What You Build

Large construction and infrastructure projects move fast, and most of what happens on-site never gets captured. We film progress, scale, and process as the work happens — footage that doubles as a record for your team and material for marketing.

We work around active job sites, safety requirements, and project schedules, coordinating with site supervisors on access and timing so filming never gets in the way of the work itself.

What We Capture

From the first site visit to the final wrap-up video, here's how we put your project on camera.

Progress Documentation

Periodic site visits captured over the life of a project, so you have a visual record of how the work came together from start to finish.

Drone & Aerial Footage

Aerial shots that show scale and layout in a way ground-level footage can't — useful for progress updates and final project videos alike.

Safety & Training Video

Clear, reusable safety walkthroughs, equipment training, and onboarding videos for crews and new hires.

Tradeshow & Bid Marketing

Polished project footage your team can use in bid packages, pitch meetings, and on the show floor — real proof of completed work.

Company Culture & Recruiting

Crew interviews and behind-the-scenes footage that help with recruiting in a competitive hiring market for trades.

Social Media Content

Short-form cuts — time-lapses, before-and-afters, milestone moments — built for Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.
