Freelance Editors & Shooters Wanted

We're a Plant City video production team looking to bring on talented freelancers for ongoing project work across Central Florida. If you edit, shoot, or both — we'd love to see your work.

What We're Hiring For

Editor

You cut footage we shoot. Commercials, training videos, social clips, podcast episodes, event recaps. Comfortable in Premiere Pro. Clean cuts, clean audio, good color, on-brand graphics.

Shooter / Videographer

You bring the camera (or use ours), show up on time, and capture the footage we need. Commercials, events, interviews, b-roll, on-site training shoots. Drone experience is a plus. Reliable transportation across Central Florida is a must.

Can you do both? Even better — let us know in the form below. We work with people who specialize and people who handle the whole pipeline.

What We're Looking For

  • Proven work — a reel, portfolio, or sample projects we can actually watch
  • Reliable communication and timelines you actually hit
  • Comfortable working as a true 1099 contractor
  • Must be willing to travel within Central Florida - travel will be compensated
  • For editors: turnaround you can commit to, and clean files we can hand off cleanly
  • For shooters: your own gear, backup gear, and the judgment to handle a shoot day
