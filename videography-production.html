Video Production Questions

How much does video production cost?

It depends on how many shoot days, crew members, and finished videos your project needs. We give you a clear written quote before anything is filmed. For TV spots, our guide to what a commercial costs in Tampa Bay walks through the numbers.

Do you handle planning and scripting?

Yes. We take projects from the first idea through scripting, shot planning, filming, editing, and final delivery, and we plan shoot day with you ahead of time.

Can you get our commercial on TV or streaming?

Yes. As an official Spectrum partner, we produce the spot and place it on local cable and streaming platforms so it reaches the customers you want.

Do you film corporate and industrial projects?

Yes. We film training, safety, and company videos on location at offices, job sites, and industrial facilities. See our construction and industrial video production page for examples.

Where in Florida do you film?

We're based in Plant City and film most often in Tampa, Lakeland, Brandon, and across Hillsborough and Polk County. We travel farther for larger projects. See our Tampa video production and Lakeland video production pages for local work.

Do you offer drone video?

Yes. Our pilots are FAA Part 107 certified and can add aerial footage to any project, from commercials to property tours and outdoor events.

How long does it take to produce a video?

A commercial or short-form video usually goes from concept to delivery in a couple of weeks. Multi-day shoots and projects with several review rounds take longer, and we give you a realistic timeline once we know the scope.

What industries do you make video for?

We produce video for HVAC and home service companies, construction and industrial companies, attractions and theme parks, farms and agritourism businesses, and real estate agents, along with healthcare, retail, restaurants, schools, nonprofits, and automotive businesses.

Do you offer a flat-price TV commercial?

Yes. Our $999 TV commercial special is a complete 30-second commercial, filmed and edited by our team and delivered ready to air on local cable and broadcast TV.

What do I get at the end of a video project?

You get the final edited files in every format you need, such as a full-length version plus shorter cuts for social media, with graphics, color correction, and audio mixing finished. Commercials come with broadcast and streaming-ready exports.
