Types of Training Video We Produce

If your team needs to learn it, we can film it. Here's a closer look at the business videos our clients ask for most.

Onboarding & Training Series

An onboarding video series gives every new hire the same strong first day — no matter who's training that week or how busy the floor is. Instead of a rushed verbal walkthrough, new employees watch a clear, consistent introduction to your company, your expectations, and how things actually work.

We break onboarding into short, focused episodes — company overview, role basics, tools and systems, day-one expectations — so new staff can learn at a steady pace and rewatch anything they missed. A training series also scales: hire one person or twenty, the onboarding cost is already covered.

HR & Policy Videos

When a policy changes, you need every employee to actually receive the same message — not a game of telephone across shifts and departments. HR and policy videos let you communicate updates clearly and consistently without scheduling an all-hands meeting.

We produce videos covering company policies, benefits explanations, code-of-conduct expectations, workplace procedures, and annual HR updates. Because it's recorded, HR has a clear, on-the-record version of every policy communication — and new hires get the same information as everyone else from day one.

Safety & Compliance Training

Safety and compliance training is too important to depend on who's delivering it that day. A video makes the training consistent, repeatable, and documented — every employee sees the exact same procedures demonstrated the exact same way.

We film safety and compliance videos on-site at your facility, so the training shows your real equipment, your real work areas, and your real procedures — not generic stock footage. That makes the training easier to follow and far more relevant. Recorded compliance training also gives you a clear record that every employee was trained on current procedures.

How-To Videos for Teams & Roles

How-to videos are the ones your team rewatches — the step-by-step guides for a specific task, a piece of equipment, or a software system. Instead of interrupting an experienced employee every time someone forgets a step, your team has a clear reference they can pull up on their own.

We produce role-specific and task-specific how-to videos for everything from operating equipment to following a process correctly to using the software your business runs on. Each one is short, focused, and easy to find — so the right answer is always one video away.

How It Works

We keep it simple and we come to you.

  • We talk through what your team needs to learn and map out the video series
  • We film on-site at your location — minimal disruption to your day
  • We edit clear cuts with captions, graphics, and chapter markers
  • You get final files ready for your LMS, intranet, or a private link
  • Need an update later? We can re-cut or add segments without starting over
