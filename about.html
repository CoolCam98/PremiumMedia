A Local Video, Marketing & Photography Team in Plant City

We're a small team that handles video, web, and photography for local businesses, schools, and organizations. We're not an agency — we're the people who actually do the work.

Who We Are

Premium Media started because we saw local businesses in Plant City paying big-agency prices for work that didn't feel personal. We thought: what if you could get that same level of quality from a team that actually knows your name and cares about your business?

We're small on purpose. We handle video production, web design, digital marketing, and photography in-house — which means your website, commercials, social media, and brand photos all work together instead of feeling like they were made by five different companies.

What We Do

We keep it simple. Here's what we can help with — and if you're not sure which one you need, that's fine. We'll help you figure it out.

Video Production

Commercials, training videos, event coverage, interviews, testimonials, social media clips — if it involves a camera, we handle it. Most clients have never done a video project before, and we make it easy.

Digital Marketing & Web

Websites that actually work on mobile, show up on Google, and make it easy for customers to contact you. We also help with social media content and branding when you need it.

Photography

Event photography, professional headshots, branding sessions, and a limited number of weddings each year. Natural photos that look like you — not like a stock image.

How We Work

No surprises, no upsells, no disappearing after the deposit clears.

Straight talk, every time

We won't push you into a package you don't need. We look at where your business is right now, what you're trying to do, and what will actually move the needle.

If something doesn't make sense for you, we'll say so — even if it means we make less money. That's just how we operate.

People usually reach out when they:

  • Know they need help but aren't sure where to start
  • Want to work with real people, not a faceless agency
  • Are tired of paying for stuff that doesn't work
  • Need someone local who actually picks up the phone

Want to talk? Just reach out.

You don't need to have a full project plan ready. Tell us a little about your business and what you're thinking — we'll take it from there. No sales pitch, no obligation.
